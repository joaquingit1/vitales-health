HealthBench source and numerical verification

Catalog source: L02, openai/simple-evals.
Repository: https://github.com/openai/simple-evals
Commit: 652c89d0ca9df547706735883097e9537d40dc47
Primary files: healthbench_eval.py, common.py, types.py,
sampler/chat_completion_sampler.py. Original MIT license is retained.
The original source bytes and their SHA-256 values are in vendor/SOURCE.json.
The scorer is packaged without source edits because upstream has no wheel metadata.

Dataset URLs, exact byte counts, example counts and measured SHA-256 values
are recorded in vendor/SOURCE.json and healthbench-plan.json. The three files
were independently fetched and their bytes reverified on 2026-11-15.
Main: 5000 examples. Hard: 1000 examples. Consensus: 3671 examples.
All records passed the generated boundary schema. Private local projections
contain prompt_id and prompt only. No patient example or rubric is committed.

Grader sampler values come from healthbench_eval.py physician_completions_main
and ChatCompletionSampler.__init__ at the pinned commit: the selected model,
system message, output token bound and sampler temperature are recorded in
vendor/SOURCE.json and the versioned plan. A changed dataset, grader or harness
is modified. It is never silently labeled official.

NASA does not define this external benchmark or its scoring algorithm.
The numerical protocol fixture checks positive and negative point arithmetic
against a separately calculated one-third score. This verifies computation,
not clinical correctness. It supplies no clinical golden expectation.
