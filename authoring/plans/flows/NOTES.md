# Flow plans: what was left out and why

Twenty plans, one per flow in config/responder-flows.json. Written by model:claude-sonnet-5-5, review_state unreviewed, checked_by null.

## Choices that apply to every plan

* Environments: all four for every flow. No flow is environment specific. Where a flow item only makes sense with or without gravity, the step carries `environments` (weightless: station_leo, transit; partial gravity: lunar_surface, mars_surface) and the gap the flow itself leaves ("to be set by clinical review") is written as a step saying so.
* EVA: every flow has an `eva` environment note. EVA is not one of the four plan environments, so no EVA text is in any plan. Those notes (bring the person inside, spacewalk emergency procedure, flight rules) stay in the flow.
* Supplies: `needs.supplies` is `[]` everywhere. Items that need a kit supply were either written so the crew can do them without it or left out (per flow below). Equipment the kit map does not list (AED, habitat sensors, radiation sensors, app timer, app clock) is written without any `needs`, so those steps always show as aboard.
* Blood sugar meter: the kit-map instrument `glucose` is in no environment's kit, so the "blood sugar, only if the kit carries a meter" measurement is left out of unresponsive_breathing, fainting, seizure and low_blood_sugar (a step needing it would be not aboard everywhere and has no instrument free substitute that measures sugar). The flows keep it.
* Instruments used (camera_station, pulse_oximeter, bp_cuff, thermometer) are in all four kits, so no substitute steps were needed. Every plan also has an any_crew step with no instrument or skill in do_now or watch.
* Sources: a step drawn from a flow treatment step cites that step's source_ids and is `sourced`. Steps drawn from questions, measurements, plain, immediate steps or call_ground_when carry no source of their own in the flow, so they are `source_ids: []` and `team default`. Only flow source_ids are cited; no NASA catalog source outside the flow was added.
* No thresholds or intervals invented. Where a flow says a level or time is to be set by clinical review or flight rules, the step says that. Rechecks (cadence null, to_be_set_by clinical review) appear only on chest_pain, severe_abdominal_pain, head_injury and eye_injury, where the flow asks for a repeat without an interval.
* Immediate steps are not copied wholesale: the help flow runs them. They appear again only where a treatment step in the flow covers the same action. Calling the CMO, alarms and "call every crewmate" are help flow actions and are not repeated.
* if_worse: one step per plan opens help on its own flow. Other if_worse steps are plain statements. Switching to another flow cannot be opened from a plan step, so those steps say "go back to the first screen" (not breathing) or "go back to the first screen of Someone needs help and choose <flow title>" (DCS, allergic reaction, Toxic air).
* Voice, video and live consultation items in the flows' ground treatment steps are left out everywhere (they join only while the link is up and nothing waits for them). The ground_log items carry only what to record.
* Medicine words: every step with a medicine, inhaler, adrenaline, glucose, glucagon or pain relief word, and every oxygen step meant as treatment (fluids too), has "as in the onboard procedure" and `medicine: {"named_as": ...}`. Steps that only say "as in the onboard procedure" for a non-medicine action (airway care, examination, suit pressure treatment, sugary food, haemostatic dressing, suction) have `medicine: null`.
* Modes: stabilize for most; resolve for choking, eye_injury, low_blood_sugar; talk_through for mental_health_crisis.

## Per flow: left out

* not_breathing: face shield or pocket mask (rescue breaths step has no kit need); crew medical restraint system (position step starts "If the kit lists a crew medical restraint system, strap the person to it first"); AED (no kit-map instrument, step says "If the kit lists an AED"); the part of the stop-CPR decision that involves the ground while the link is up; voice and video support; the lunar and mars partial gravity method ("to be set by clinical review", no step beyond the common one); raising the alarm and the first compressions (help flow). Blood pressure after recovery is a medical_officer step because the flow says the CMO decides when.
* unresponsive_breathing: blood sugar meter (see above); oxygen supply "if listed" (MO step "as in the onboard procedure", no need); whether the recovery position works as on Earth in partial gravity (to be set by clinical review, flow gives nothing to write); how to hold or strap them in weightlessness is a "to be set by clinical review" step.
* choking: how well Earth technique works in partial gravity (no step); bracing method in weightlessness is a "to be set by clinical review" step; questions "can you speak or cough" and "did the object come out" (help flow, asked of the person while acting); the ground log is routine unless they stopped responding.
* severe_bleeding: tourniquet (kit supply "if listed"; the immediate step runs it, the plan keeps only "write down the time any tourniquet went on"); gloves and dressings (written as "a dressing or a clean cloth"); gauze or suction for floating blood (crew step says gauze or a clean cloth, suction only in the MO step without a need); haemostatic dressing (MO step without a need); where-on-the-body-map and what-caused-it questions (in the ground_log item); camera pulse only if the person already sits still.
* chest_pain: AED (immediate step); opening the DCS flow after a spacewalk (written as "go back to the first screen of Someone needs help and choose Decompression sickness after a spacewalk", cites RS49); sitting posture in lunar and mars gravity beyond the common rest step; ECG and blood tests (not aboard, named in the ground_log item and in `cannot_replace_text` "It cannot replace an ECG."); the link may be down for a day on the Moon (the plan aboard already runs without it).
* breathing_difficulty: opening the Toxic air, DCS and Allergic reaction flows (written as "go back to the first screen of Someone needs help and choose <flow title>", plus the first screen for not breathing); reliever inhaler "if listed" and oxygen supply "if listed" (MO steps without a need); dust notes for the Moon and Mars (no step; no instruction in the flow beyond the warning).
* fainting: blood sugar meter (see above); weightless positioning is a "to be set by clinical review" step; standing blood pressure is a medical_officer step because the flow says only when the CMO asks, and "the change that matters" stays "to be set by clinical review".
* seizure: soft padding (written "something soft", no need); blood sugar meter (see above); the app timer is a plain note step with no need; real time specialist consultation (ground item left out); the "give no food or drink" step has no source in the flow.
* head_injury: cold pack and dressings (written "something cold" and "a clean dressing", no need); the light for pupils (camera photo only); ground video with real time audio (left out; the log item says the video goes with the log); the not responding branch is a plain if_worse step, not an opening step.
* eye_injury: eye shield, eye wash set, drink bag, towels, rinse water as supplies (the example plan used supplies; this one cannot): shield written as "any cover must rest on the bone, not the eye", rinse written as "plenty of clean water", weightless rinse as "soak up the floating water as you go"; test card and distance for the sight check (to be set by clinical review); contact lenses question kept inside the ask step.
* burns: cling film, burn dressing, water and wet towels (written "cover loosely with something clean", "cool water or a wet towel"); weightless and partial gravity cooling methods ("to be set by clinical review" steps); fire response (help flow, immediate step 0); the question "is the face, mouth or throat burned" is the watch step; electrical and chemical burn warnings are in the opening if_worse step.
* toxic_exposure: portable breathing apparatus or masks (written "keep masks on", no need); water and eye wash set (written "plenty of water"); habitat sensors (no kit-map instrument, step has no need); the station ammonia alarm response and hatches (immediate steps); safe haven and habitat layout on the Moon and Mars ("to be set by flight rules", no step); when crew outside come back in (EVA).
* pressure_loss: suits, masks and pressure gauge (supplies; the response step is the vehicle procedure text with no need); habitat sensors (no need); station and Moon and Mars habitat procedures (Moon and Mars "to be set by flight rules", no step); the first steps (warn, gather, safe haven, suits) are help flow steps repeated in short as the vehicle response.
* dcs_after_eva: oxygen mask "if listed" and suit and oxygen for treatment (MO steps without a need); prebreathe and suit and habitat pressures on the Moon and Mars ("set by flight rules", no step); no hyperbaric chamber note (no step); real time audio and video advice (left out).
* solar_particle_event: stowage bags and supplies (written "stowage and equipment mass", no need); habitat radiation sensors and personal tracker (no kit-map instrument, no need); the level that calls for shelter and how long to stay (flight rules, shown as text only); Moon shelter covered with lunar soil only on lunar_surface; Mars shelter design ("to be set by flight rules", no step); station and transit shielding notes (no step); postponing outside activities.
* severe_allergic_reaction: adrenaline auto-injector "if listed" (MO step, no need); who may give it when the CMO cannot (kept inside the MO step as "to be set by clinical review", so no crew step gives it); the second auto-injector after 5 minutes comes from an immediate step and has no source of its own; voice, video and telemetry support (left out).
* low_blood_sugar: blood sugar meter (see above, left out); food stores (written "from the food stores" with no need; the sugary step has the onboard procedure phrase and `medicine: null` because it is food); amount of sugar is not written ("as in the onboard procedure"); injection when they cannot swallow is the MO step.
* severe_abdominal_pain: tests this kit lacks (named in the ground_log item and in `cannot_replace_text` "It cannot replace the tests this kit lacks."); the flow's T2 "check it another way" step belongs to the CMO in the flow, the three readings are any_crew steps in check_another_way and the CMO decision on further care is in medical_officer; no pain medicine until the CMO gives it ("as in the onboard procedure", medicine set).
* mental_health_crisis: restraint as a last resort sits only in the MO step; behavioral health consultants and a private conference (kept as one ground_log line "when the link allows", never a step); whether a crewmate in crisis takes part in a spacewalk (EVA, flight rules); pulse and blood pressure only if the person agrees, so one optional step needing the oximeter and the cuff.
* something_else: nothing else is in the flow beyond assessment and the readings; the camera pulse step applies only if the person already sits still.

## Revision after the independent checker (pass 4)

Applied to the 15 rejected plans, and where the same cause applied elsewhere:

* Availability conditions kept in step text, exactly as the flow states them, with `needs.supplies` still `[]`: oxygen supply (unresponsive_breathing, breathing_difficulty, toxic_exposure), oxygen mask and suit (dcs_after_eva), masks, suits and oxygen masks (toxic_exposure, pressure_loss), face shield, restraint system and AED (not_breathing), haemostatic dressing and suction (severe_bleeding), burn dressing and cling film (burns), adrenaline auto-injector and a second one (severe_allergic_reaction, plus a new first do_now step alg_get_injector), glucose or glucagon for injection (low_blood_sugar), habitat sensors (toxic_exposure, pressure_loss), radiation sensors and personal tracker (solar_particle_event).
* Prerequisites restored: restraint before compressions, and weightless holds only on station_leo and transit with a new partial gravity step "to be set by clinical review" (not_breathing); recovery position only when breathing is normal, otherwise compressions, with the partial gravity caveat (unresponsive_breathing, seizure); choking blows and thrusts only when they cannot breathe, cough or make a noise, otherwise keep coughing; camera pulse only if already sitting still, never move them, never delay care, on repeat readings too (chest_pain, severe_abdominal_pain).
* Routing: chest_pain and breathing_difficulty now say "go back to the first screen of Someone needs help and choose <flow title>" for DCS, Toxic air and Severe allergic reaction. breathing_difficulty gains a first do_now air check and an if_worse DCS step.
* burns cover step is exactly "cling film or a clean dressing, if the kit lists one" (no longer anything clean).
* Readings moved from check_another_way to watch in not_breathing, choking, severe_bleeding, breathing_difficulty, burns, toxic_exposure, pressure_loss and severe_allergic_reaction, so the action to take at once is the first stage a plan shows. (Superseded in pass 5 below: every plan except something_else now keeps readings in watch.)

## Revision after the independent checker (pass 5)

* No plan except something_else has a check_another_way stage any more. Every measurement and check is in `watch`, after the original watch steps, and ends with "Never delay or stop <the urgent care of that flow> for this." Seizure readings start "Only after the jerking stops." and the eye sight check and photo start "Only once the rinse is done." The chest_pain and severe_abdominal_pain `cannot_replace_text` stays on their readings. something_else keeps its readings in check_another_way because that flow tells the crew to take the basic readings while they stay with the person.
* do_now now starts with what the flow's immediate steps say: unresponsive_breathing starts with breathing surveillance (moved out of watch); seizure starts with noting the time; fainting starts with the breathing check and lies them down only if breathing normally; chest_pain has the spacewalk routing in do_now right after rest (removed from if_worse); pressure_loss has the DCS routing in do_now (the reopen step now only says "if anyone gets worse"); dcs_after_eva gains treat-as-DCS and full pressure (oxygen mask if the kit lists one) in do_now; low_blood_sugar sugary food step starts "help them sit down"; severe_abdominal_pain has the red-flag call step in do_now right after rest (the reopening step stays in if_worse).
* Prerequisites and conditions: severe_bleeding presses around an embedded object, never on it, and the replacement dressing is "if the kit lists one, otherwise a clean cloth"; head_injury cold only if they are awake, clean dressing only if the kit lists one; breathing_difficulty masks only if the kit lists them.

## Revision after the independent checker (pass 6)

Only the nine rejected plans changed.

* not_breathing and severe_bleeding: the medical officer steps for an airway device, venous access, medicines and fluids are now "If the kit lists ..." with a stated fallback ("keep up compressions", "keep pressing on the wound"). severe_allergic_reaction: further medicines, oxygen and fluids likewise, with the fallback "keep the position and keep watching their breathing". None of these items is in the kit map, so `needs` stays empty.
* breathing_difficulty: the Severe allergic reaction and spacewalk or pressure loss routings are now do_now steps right after sitting upright, no longer in if_worse.
* head_injury: do_now order is the unresponsive branch (assume a neck injury, first screen), keep still, cold if awake, the first alertness check, then the questions. The unresponsive step left if_worse. The repeated alertness check stays in watch with its recheck.
* burns: brushing off chemical powder is the first do_now step; cooling starts "Once any chemical powder is brushed off".
* toxic_exposure: "Protect yourself first: keep out of the affected air" is unconditional; the mask is "if the kit lists masks", and the mask on the person is "if the kit lists one".
* pressure_loss: the station step reads the sensors to work out the reserve time "if the habitat has sensors", and otherwise says retreat to the safe haven now. The low oxygen signs watch moved into do_now before the oxygen step.
* low_blood_sugar: new first do_now step: if they do not respond or cannot swallow safely, give nothing by mouth and go back to the first screen. Sugary food is only for someone fully alert who can swallow safely.
