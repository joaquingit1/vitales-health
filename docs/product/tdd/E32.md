# E32: Treat here: supplies and planning

**Technical design document brief · All scope belongs to the hackathon**

Owner stream: A for uses, cover and the what-if (E32-02, E32-04), with stream B for the supplies list and the cover rule (E32-01, E32-03) and stream D for the kit aboard card and the Plan here list (E32-05). Dependency stage: S3. Depends on: E27 E28.

## Product contract

Requirements: R57 R58 R59. Shared entities: SupplyStock, SupplyUse, SupplyCoverView. Read [PRD](../PRD.md) (requirements R57, R58, R59), [pitch](../PITCH.md) (the six acts), [use cases](../USE-CASES.md) (UC25), [contracts](../CONTRACTS.md) (section 21, kit map, section 22, onboard plans, and section 25, supplies and cover; endpoints in `api/openapi.yaml`), [interaction design](../UX-DESIGN.md) (section 3.6, This kit) and [validation](../VALIDATION.md) (scenarios V83, V91 and V92; demo assertion D32) before designing.

The rule in one line: each plan step declares the instruments, supplies and skills it needs; availability and cover are computed per environment, stock and roster, and the planning view shows what to pack and whom to train. The ground is a reader of the log, never a step anything waits on. Every step, threshold and phrase comes from a versioned configuration document; clinical text is the team's reading of public sources, labeled “Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review” until a clinician checks it, and medicine steps say “as in the onboard procedure” with no dose.

## Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze the supplies list and stock per environment in the kit map, uses written by plan steps, cover computed from stock and the plans' declared rate, the cover warning, and the planning what-if that adds supplies and training beside instruments and writes nothing. Decide how a declared rate is written for a step, how a refused use shows its substitute, the cover threshold as a value to be set by the flight surgeon, and how the what-if bounds its inputs so it never writes. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

## Required work and acceptance

### E32-01: List the supplies and stock per environment in the kit map

Acceptance: kit-map.json lists supplies with units and stock per environment; only items the sources or the team name, each with its basis.

### E32-02: Write supply uses from plan steps and compute cover

Acceptance: Plan steps write supply uses; cover is computed from stock and the plans' declared rate, never typed; a use that would go below zero is refused and the step shows Not available with its substitute (EI12, V91).

### E32-03: Write the supply cover warning rule

Acceptance: Cover below [threshold to be set by the flight surgeon] raises Keep an eye on it, raised by a rule (V91).

### E32-04: Extend the what-if to supplies and training

Acceptance: The what-if accepts supplies and training beside instruments and returns ring changes, the plans that lose a dependency on the medical officer and cover changes; nothing is written (V92).

### E32-05: Build the kit aboard card and the Plan here list

Acceptance: The kit aboard card lists instruments, supplies with their cover and trained hands as counts; the kit view's Plan here list shows each row's plan summary and what the ground was for; all of it from the API (D32).
