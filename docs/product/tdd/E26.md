The rule in one line: help is one tap away, always, and its first steps never wait for the model, a sensor or the ground. Who, then for a crewmate responding and breathing (not sure counts as no), then the emergency steps or “What happened?” and “Do this now”, with the assistant beside the steps, never in front of them, guiding only from reviewed procedures and the kit. The helper sees the person's latest readings and the allergies and medicines on record, never the journal. The ground message leaves only after a helper approves. Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review before live guidance.

## Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze the responder flows and their review state, the who, responding and breathing checks, the help-session access grant, the crew broadcast and the ground message states; keep every fixed step free of the model. Decide how the fixed screens work before authentication completes, how a second press for the same person joins the open session, how a red-flag answer in a concern opens a session and moves the episode's urgency, how each assistant turn is checked against the pinned flow and the kit list before it is shown, and how the help log becomes the ground message and its HandoffSnapshot. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

## Required work and acceptance

### E26-01: Implement the help session and the fixed responder flows

Acceptance: Who, responding and breathing checks, then the emergency steps or "What happened?" and "Do this now", all served from versioned flows built from RESPONDER-FLOWS.md with no model, sensor or ground call; not sure counts as no; an active Act now or Emergency habitat warning or a vehicle alarm puts the air-safety line first; each step logged with its hub time; flows carry their review state.

### E26-02: Implement the guided help session within reviewed steps and the kit

Acceptance: The assistant asks one question at a time beside the fixed steps and never before them; every suggestion cites a reviewed procedure step or a kit item; a turn naming a medicine or dose the procedure does not contain is rejected and logged; stopping the model leaves every fixed step working.

### E26-03: Build the help screens, the helper's readings and the crew broadcast

Acceptance: The button is in the stage head of every screen and opens within 500 ms with the model stopped; the helper sees the person's latest readings with sources and the allergies and medicines on record, never the journal, and access ends with the session; an emergency shows on every crew screen with who needs help, who is helping and where; a second helper joins the same session and log.

### E26-04: Implement the ground message from the help log

Acceptance: Drafted from the log as the session runs; drafted, approved, queued and sent in that order after a helper approves; the current environment's delay and link state shown; the session never waits for a reply; no journal or participation content.
