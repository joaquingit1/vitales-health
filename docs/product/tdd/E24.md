Read [PRD](../PRD.md) (sections 4.10 and 9), [use cases](../USE-CASES.md) (sections 1 and 4, UC04, UC05, UC11, UC16 and UC17), [contracts](../CONTRACTS.md) (section 11 “Configuration documents and the no-hardcoded-data rule”, section 14 “Warnings”, section 17 “Assistant conversation” and section 18 “Event stream”; endpoints in `api/openapi.yaml`), [LLM architecture](../LLM-ARCHITECTURE.md) and [validation](../VALIDATION.md) (scenarios V55 to V58, V65, V69, V70, V72, V74 and V75; demo assertions D16, D17, D19 and D22) before designing. The reference screens are To do, Needs attention and the warning detail of [design/interaction-prototype.html](../design/interaction-prototype.html).

The rule in one line: every warning says who raised it. Four levels (“For your information”, “Keep an eye on it”, “Act now”, “Emergency”), `raised_by` rule, sensor_limit or assistant, evidence, regions and numbered steps; states raised, acknowledged and cleared with a reason. “Act now” and “Emergency” never depend on the model, and the assistant can never lower, clear or acknowledge a warning that a rule or a sensor limit raised. Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review before live guidance.

## Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze the four levels and their words, who may raise each, the clear conditions and reasons, the hold during sleep and walks, and the bounds on assistant insights. Decide how a person's answer reaches the engine as a rule, how a higher level supersedes a lower one without lowering in place, how warnings reach every crew screen through the event stream, how the stub model exercises the rejected cases, and how the vehicle's own alarm state is read without ever being acknowledged or silenced. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

## Required work and acceptance

### E24-01: Implement the warning record and lifecycle

Acceptance: Four levels with their words; raised_by rule, sensor_limit or assistant, with evidence, regions and numbered steps; raised, acknowledged and cleared, where acknowledging records who and when and leaves it open and clearing records its reason; a level never drops in place; no state or endpoint lets the assistant clear, lower or acknowledge a rule or sensor warning.

### E24-02: Implement the rule and sensor-limit raisers

Acceptance: Sensor limits, answers and help events raise warnings at the level their versioned rule or limit sets, with its ID and source; Act now and Emergency come only from rules, sensor limits or a person's answer through a rule; a quiet sensor raises "Keep an eye on it: sensor quiet" and never clears an open warning; during sleep or a walk only Emergency interrupts.

### E24-03: Bound assistant insights by level

Acceptance: An insight at For your information or Keep an eye on it names evidence that resolves to accepted records and one action; an Act now or Emergency proposal, and any attempt to lower or clear a rule or sensor warning, is rejected and logged; the person's dismissal clears only the insight; tested with the stub model.

### E24-04: Build To do warnings, Needs attention and the warning detail

Acceptance: To do lists warnings first and the stage title counts them; each warning shows what is happening, why it matters with its regions lit as alert lights, numbered steps and who raised it from what; the level is always in words; "Acknowledged, still open" after acknowledging; the clear reason is shown.
