E31: Who helps

Technical design document brief · All scope belongs to the hackathon

Owner stream: A for SkillRecord, freshness and the readiness views (E31-01, E31-05), with stream B for the role rules (E31-02) and stream D for drill mode and practice patches (E31-03, E31-04). Dependency stage: S3. Depends on: E01 E22 E25 E26.

Product contract

Requirements: R53 R54 R55. Shared entities: SkillRecord, RoleAssignment, Drill. Read PRD (requirements R53, R54, R55), pitch (the six acts), use cases (UC23 and UC24), contracts (section 8, participation, section 16, Someone needs help, and section 24, who helps: roles, skills and drills; endpoints in api/openapi.yaml), routine (the drill item), interaction design (section 5, drills, section 9, Who does what, and section 11, practice patches) and validation (scenarios V88 to V90; demo assertions D37, D41 and D44) before designing.

The rule in one line: the app knows who can do each step now and how recently they practiced it, proposes roles with a reason when someone needs help, including when the medical officer is the patient or outside, and keeps skills fresh with drills that earn practice patches, never scores. The ground is a reader of the log, never a step anything waits on. Every step, threshold and phrase comes from a versioned configuration document; clinical text is the team's reading of public sources, labeled “Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review” until a clinician checks it, and medicine steps say “as in the onboard procedure” with no dose.

Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze SkillRecord and its computed freshness; the role rules (lead helper, second helper, kit fetcher, medical officer, communicator), eligibility (never the subject, nobody in a walk overlay for an on-site role) and the medical officer cascade; drills in the routine and practice patches that count completion only. Decide how preflight training enters as Sim records, how freshness cadences are set as team defaults until the flight surgeon sets them, where role proposals sit on the help screen (always below the fixed steps), and how readiness stays out of every participation view. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

Required work and acceptance

E31-01: Implement SkillRecord and computed freshness

Acceptance: SkillRecords come from preflight training (Sim), drills and events; freshness fresh, due or stale is computed from the catalog's cadence; a person sees their own; the medical officer role sees the crew's; nothing ranks anyone.

E31-02: Write the role rules and propose roles in help sessions

Acceptance: role-rules.json defines the roles, each flow's needs, eligibility and the medical officer cascade; help sessions propose roles with a reason, always below the fixed steps; the subject never gets a role; nobody in a walk overlay gets an on-site role; every role can be changed (EI9, V88, V89, D37, D41).

E31-03: Add drills to the routine with drill mode

Acceptance: The drill routine item carries its skill IDs; drill mode runs the same teach steps and camera checks on a manikin or a crewmate's wrist; completion writes each participant's SkillRecord (V90).

E31-04: Award practice patches for completed drills

Acceptance: Practice patches count completed drills only; drill measurements are shown live and never stored, ranked or rewarded; the streak and the crew goal do not change; no practice data appears on medical screens (EI10, V90, D44).

E31-05: Serve the skills and readiness views with their privacy

Acceptance: A person's own skills and the medical officer's crew readiness show skills and freshness only, never health values; readiness never appears in participation views.

TDD completion checklist

Specify API payloads and errors; state transitions and invariants; persistence/index choices; authorization and consent checks; unit/time/provenance handling; concurrency, idempotency and cancellation; resource limits and degradation; observability without private-content leakage; fixture IDs and acceptance evidence; dependencies and integration examples. Include a sequence diagram for the principal success path and a failure path. Name unresolved clinical or equipment assumptions explicitly.

Handoff to implementation

Produce the technical design document, concrete contract examples, implementation task breakdown and meaningful tests. Use the frozen shared contracts; propose changes through an explicit decision record. Mark evidence as planned until tests actually run. No feature is removed solely because the team has four developers.
