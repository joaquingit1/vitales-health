Requirements: R34 R35 R41 R42 R43. Shared entities: EnvironmentProfile, SensorReading, SensorLimit. Read [PRD](../PRD.md) (section 4.8), [use cases](../USE-CASES.md) (section 3, UC04 to UC07, UC12 and UC17), [environments](../ENVIRONMENTS.md) and [environment-hazards.json](../environment-hazards.json), [contracts](../CONTRACTS.md) (section 11 “Configuration documents and the no-hardcoded-data rule”, section 12 “Environments, hazards and mission context” and section 13 “Sensors and readings”; endpoints in `api/openapi.yaml`), [LLM architecture](../LLM-ARCHITECTURE.md) and [validation](../VALIDATION.md) (scenarios V55 to V59, V66, V70, V72, V73 and V75; demo assertions D16, D18 and D19) before designing. The reference screens are the Habitat card and the Habitat screen of [design/interaction-prototype.html](../design/interaction-prototype.html), with the environment data shapes in [design/crew-data.js](../design/crew-data.js).

The rule in one line: the app knows where it is. One confirmed environment (`station_leo`, `transit`, `lunar_surface` or `mars_surface`) and each person's overlays (`eva`, `post_eva`, `exercise`, `sleep`) set the hazards watched, the sensors that matter and the ground delay. Every limit and effect comes from a versioned configuration document built from ENVIRONMENTS.md, with its source, a labeled team default or “threshold to be set by clinical review”; this brief defines no number. Every channel runs as Live, Replay or Sim and says so, and a quiet channel is never shown as current or safe. Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review before live guidance.

## Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze the environment and overlay vocabulary, the limit table built from ENVIRONMENTS.md, the sensor adapters and replay bindings, and each channel's expected reporting interval. Decide how a mission phase event proposes a change and how the crew confirms it, how the demo control is recorded as a demo control, how a replay series is bound to a channel and its replay clock shown, how ppm converts to partial pressure with the real cabin pressure, how freshness is computed, which habitat sensor modules (if any) run Live at the event, and how the level each limit raises is settled with clinical review (USE-CASES.md shows the CO2 one-hour limit at “Keep an eye on it”; environment-hazards.json maps it to “Act now”). Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

## Required work and acceptance

### E23-01: Implement the environment profile, activity overlays and confirmed changes

Acceptance: One active profile per mission among station_leo, transit, lunar_surface and mars_surface; a change takes effect only after a crew member confirms it and is a new record with its source; eva, post_eva, exercise and sleep overlays are per person with start and end; no reading carries over as current after a change.

### E23-02: Implement habitat sensor adapters with Live, Replay, Sim and stale states

Acceptance: The Rodent Research-1 CO2 and RadLab dose-rate series feed their channels labeled Replay with their original dates; Sim channels say Sim; a channel quiet past its expected interval shows "No data since" with the last time and is never shown as current or safe; one reading per adapter sequence; no Replay or Sim reading becomes anyone's exposure.

### E23-03: Serve the limit table, hazards and environment-aware adverse effects from versioned configuration documents

Acceptance: Each limit carries its level, window, unit and source ID from ENVIRONMENTS.md, a labeled team default, or "threshold to be set by clinical review", which evaluates nothing; effects and regions come from the active environment's hazard entry with their source IDs; Mars dust names perchlorate salts and Moon dust does not; editing a limit in its configuration document changes the screen with no client change.

### E23-04: Build the Habitat card and the Habitat screen from served data

Acceptance: The Habitat card shows the environment, the ground delay and the sensors that matter against their limits; the Habitat screen shows each sensor's reading, source word, trend with its limit line, effects with their regions lit and what to do; a stale sensor shows "No data since"; "Living here" lists the place's hazards; nothing on either is held in client code.
