## Product contract

Requirements: R51 R52. Shared entities: Skill, GuidedCheck, CoachingSample. Read [PRD](../PRD.md) (requirements R51, R52), [pitch](../PITCH.md) (the six acts), [use cases](../USE-CASES.md) (UC22), [contracts](../CONTRACTS.md) (section 23, teach and check; endpoints in `api/openapi.yaml`), [interaction design](../UX-DESIGN.md) (section 7, A crewmate checks, and section 9, Someone needs help) and [validation](../VALIDATION.md) (scenarios V85 to V87 and V96; demo assertion D38; the venue study in section 6) before designing.

The rule in one line: when a step needs hands, the app teaches a crewmate one step at a time on the body figure and, where a camera can verify it, the camera array checks the crewmate's work; the dashed ring stays. The ground is a reader of the log, never a step anything waits on. Every step, threshold and phrase comes from a versioned configuration document; clinical text is the team's reading of public sources, labeled “Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review” until a clinician checks it, and medicine steps say “as in the onboard procedure” with no dose.

## Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze the skills catalog (teach steps with short and first-time text, landmarks, checks with sourced targets and tolerances with their basis, phrases); the GuidedCheck states and outcomes (agree, disagree, no reference); the rule that a guided check never confirms or excludes a condition and that disagreement writes no value; the experimental label on every camera check until the venue study. Decide the count agreement tolerance as a team default to be set by clinical review, how beats are timed on the hub, how the camera pulse window aligns with the count, the quality gate for the compression rate, and how a live demo reading goes to the demo-operator record. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

## Required work and acceptance

### E30-01: Write the skills catalog with teach steps, landmarks and checks

Acceptance: skills-catalog.json holds the pulse check at the wrist, chest compressions, pressure on a bleed, eye rinse and the recovery position, each with short and first-time teach steps, landmarks, checks with sourced targets and tolerances with their basis, phrases and sources; unreviewed until a clinician checks it.

### E30-02: Implement the GuidedCheck engine

Acceptance: A guided check records each Beat with its hub time, the helper's answer, the camera reference and the result agree, disagree or no reference using the catalog's tolerance; disagreement writes no value; a guided check never confirms or excludes a condition and never closes a dashed ring (EI8, V85, V86, V87, D38).

### E30-03: Use the camera pulse as the reference for a crewmate's count

Acceptance: The camera pulse taken during the count is the reference; agreement within [tolerance] writes an Observation with method crewmate_palpation, its operator and its subject; with no camera reading the count is kept and labeled unchecked; labeled Experimental until the venue check (V85 to V87).

### E30-04: Measure the compression rate from the helper's motion, experimental

Acceptance: The rate shows only when its quality gate passes; hands out of view gives can't see the hands and no number; the phrases come from the catalog; nothing on the help card animates; labeled Experimental and kept out of the video until the manikin check (V96).

### E30-05: Build the A crewmate checks screen and the landmark lights

Acceptance: The Measurement screen's A crewmate checks form shows one teach step at a time verbatim from the catalog, lights the landmark with the existing light, offers Beat with the seconds left and the regular question, and shows the engine's result; a person who never did it completes the pulse check from the screen alone in usability review (D38).

### E30-06: Run the venue study of crewmate counts and the CPR rate

Acceptance: With the protocol and consent wording frozen first, crewmate counts against the cameras on [N] consenting adult pairs and the compression rate on a manikin against a metronome are measured at the venue; agreement, disagreement and no-reading counts are published with raw results.
