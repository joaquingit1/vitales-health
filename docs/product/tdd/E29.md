**Technical design document brief · All scope belongs to the hackathon**

Owner stream: A for the link, the independence check, the catch-up log and the offline cache (E29-01, E29-02, E29-04, E29-06), with stream B for the ground-silence rule (E29-03) and stream D for the Habitat line and the For the ground card (E29-05). Dependency stage: S2. Depends on: E16 E23.

The rule in one line: the whole health loop runs on board: with the link up, delayed or down, every plan, flow, warning and routine list is the same except the ground text, and the ground receives a log when the link allows. The ground is a reader of the log, never a step anything waits on. Every step, threshold and phrase comes from a versioned configuration document; clinical text is the team's reading of public sources, labeled “Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review” until a clinician checks it, and medicine steps say “as in the onboard procedure” with no dose.

## Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze the GroundLink fields (status up, delayed or down; one-way delay; next and last contact; gap kind; gap planned; source), the rule that nothing reads the link except the ground stage, the Habitat line and the ground-silence warning, the catch-up log with its leaves_at time, and the offline cache of plans and flows on every crew device. Decide how the byte comparison of responses in the three link states is run and published, how the static scan finds readers of the link, how long the catch-up log looks back, and how a device knows its cached release is current. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

## Required work and acceptance

### E29-01: Serve the GroundLink fields and the demo control

Acceptance: GroundLink carries status up, delayed or down, the one-way delay, next and last contact, gap kind, gap planned and its source; the demo control sets it only in demo mode; every change is an event (D33).

### E29-02: Prove link independence with a static scan and a byte comparison

Acceptance: A static scan finds no reader of the link outside the ground stage, the Habitat line and the ground-silence rule; responses for plans, flows, warnings and routine are byte-identical with the link up, delayed and down except ground fields and ground text; the result is published (EI1, EI2, V81, D34, D43).

### E29-03: Write the ground-silence warning rule

Acceptance: Silence past the next contact plus [threshold to be set by clinical review] raises Keep an eye on it: ground out of contact longer than planned, raised by a rule; a planned gap raises nothing (V93, D35).

### E29-04: Queue the catch-up log with its leaves_at time

Acceptance: Handoffs and ground messages carry leaves_at and a catch-up (what happened, what was done, what is being watched, what advice is welcome); the For the ground card previews the draft, and pressing Tell the ground approves and queues it; it stays queued until the link allows; a ground reply is a new record and changes no step (V95, D40, D42).

### E29-05: Build the Habitat ground line and the For the ground card

Acceptance: Habitat's ground line shows the state, the delay or the next contact with its source word, and it is the only line that changes when the link drops; For the ground shows the drafted log and reads Waiting for the next contact while the link is down (D33, D42).

### E29-06: Cache the plans, teach steps and help flows on every crew device

Acceptance: Every crew device holds the active plans, teach steps and help flows with their release version; with the hub stopped, the first help screen and the active plan's steps render from cache and show that version (V97).
