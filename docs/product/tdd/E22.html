E22: Participation streak, patches and crew goal

Technical design document brief · All scope belongs to the hackathon

Owner stream: D. Dependency stage: S2. Depends on: E01 E03.

Product contract

Requirements: R31 R32 R33. Shared entities: CheckCompletion, StreakView, CrewGoalView, PatchAward. Read PRD, UX design (section 11, participation), contracts (section 8, participation), LLM architecture and validation (scenarios V41 to V48) before designing.

The rule in one line: participation rewards showing up and never the numbers. A check is complete when the symptom question, sleep and mood are answered, "prefer not to say" included; reporting a concern or updating an open episode also counts. The streak, patches and crew goal are derived deterministically from accepted check-completion events on the mission-day boundary, are recomputable at any time and are never edited by hand. The crew sees only the crew-goal total.

Decisions the TDD must settle

Fix the completion rule, mission-day boundary and clock-change policy, crew goal window and patch designs; keep the derivation free of values. Decide whether views are computed on read or materialized with a rebuild, how the derivation module is isolated so it cannot read observations, answer values, assessments or the journal, how late-synced events and identity corrections reach the views, and how the SIM scenario is reset to its frozen start state between demo runs. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

Required work and acceptance

E22-01: Implement check-completion events and deterministic participation views

Acceptance: Completion accepted only from answered symptom, sleep and mood (declined counts), a reported concern or an open-episode update; streak, longest streak and patches rebuild identically from events and the mission clock; the derivation cannot read values, outcomes or the journal; no view is writable.

E22-02: Implement the crew goal aggregate and participation privacy

Acceptance: Crew goal returns only the seven-day total and the number due; any breakdown returns AGGREGATE_ONLY; another person's streak and patches unreadable; SIM and LIVE computed separately and never mixed; participation absent from prompts, handoffs and the ground console.

E22-03: Build the check-done step, the streak card (day row and patches) and the crew goal ring and gauge

Acceptance: "Check done." ends the check with the streak and crew goal cards beside it; the Daily check card on Today shows the streak note and the crew half gauge; the streak card holds the mission day row and patch row; crew segments are checks, never people; a missed day shows as missed with no penalty screen; nothing on urgent, concern, assessment or shared-care screens; patches are our own designs.

TDD completion checklist

Specify API payloads and errors; state transitions and invariants; persistence/index choices; authorization and consent checks; unit/time/provenance handling; concurrency, idempotency and cancellation; resource limits and degradation; observability without private-content leakage; fixture IDs and acceptance evidence; dependencies and integration examples. Include a sequence diagram for the principal success path and a failure path. Name unresolved clinical or equipment assumptions explicitly.

Handoff to implementation

Produce the technical design document, concrete contract examples, implementation task breakdown and meaningful tests. Use the frozen shared contracts; propose changes through an explicit decision record. Mark evidence as planned until tests actually run. No feature is removed solely because the team has four developers.
