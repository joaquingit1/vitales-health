# E25: Routine scheduler and task guidance

**Technical design document brief · All scope belongs to the hackathon**

Owner stream: D, with stream B for task content (E25-03). Dependency stage: S2. Depends on: E01 E03 E23 E24.

## Product contract

Requirements: R37 R38 R43. Shared entities: RoutineTask, RoutineSchedule. Read [PRD](../PRD.md) (sections 1.2, 4.2 and 4.9), [use cases](../USE-CASES.md) (section 5, UC01 to UC03 and UC13), [routine](../ROUTINE.md) and [routine-schedule.json](../routine-schedule.json), [contracts](../CONTRACTS.md) (section 11 “Configuration documents and the no-hardcoded-data rule” and section 15 “Routine”; endpoints in `api/openapi.yaml`), [LLM architecture](../LLM-ARCHITECTURE.md) and [validation](../VALIDATION.md) (scenarios V51 to V54, V59, V67, V74 and V75; demo assertions D20 and D21) before designing. The reference screens are To do, the Routine screen with “Changed for now” and the task steps of [design/interaction-prototype.html](../design/interaction-prototype.html).

The rule in one line: the routine prompts measurements and tasks from time to time, explains each one in plain steps, and never scolds. Cadences are `continuous`, `daily`, `every_n_days`, `weekly`, `monthly`, `per_eva`, `after_event`, `mission_phase` and `as_needed`, from ROUTINE.md or “frequency to be set by the flight surgeon”. An event tightens a task with its reason, source and end, and the task returns to normal on its own. A missed task records “not done”; “not available” is a real answer; nothing is filled in and the streak is untouched. Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review before live guidance.

## Decisions the TDD must settle

Freeze the cadence vocabulary, due windows, trigger rules with reason, source and end, the number of misses before asking, and the task guidance format; frequencies come from ROUTINE.md or the flight surgeon. Decide how due windows move around sleep and walk overlays, how two triggers on one task combine and both stay visible, how the scheduler is recomputed from events after a restart, and how task guidance is versioned as configuration so an edited step reaches the screen with no client change. Record alternatives, the selected design, evidence, failure modes and migration/rollback impact. This is a design brief, not an assertion that the technical design is complete.

## Required work and acceptance

### E25-01: Implement the routine scheduler with cadences and due windows

Acceptance: Each cadence (continuous, daily, every_n_days, weekly, monthly, per_eva, after_event, mission_phase, as_needed) produces due windows for the person and environment; nothing becomes due during sleep or a walk; a missed occurrence records not done and moves to its next slot; not available records its reason and no value.

### E25-02: Implement event triggers that tighten a task for a stated time

Acceptance: A walk end, dust, a storm, a symptom report, a sensor warning or a new environment tightens one task with its reason, source and end; the task returns to its usual cadence on its own; an assistant change only adds a recheck or a question for the day; every active change is listed under "Changed for now".

### E25-03: Author task guidance as configuration from ROUTINE.md

Acceptance: Every task has one sentence on what it is, what you need, numbered plain steps, its region, minutes and why this often with its source ID or "frequency to be set by the flight surgeon", and carries "Unreviewed draft: needs clinician review before live guidance." until reviewed; environment cadences, readaptation and after-walk tasks included; editing a step changes the screen with no client change.

### E25-04: Build To do tasks, the Routine screen, task steps and Changed for now

Acceptance: To do shows what is due now with cadence and minutes after the warnings; a task opens to its steps with Record it, Not available and Later; the figure lights the task's region; Routine this week counts tasks done; after the configured number of misses the app asks once what is in the way, never shames, and the streak is untouched.
