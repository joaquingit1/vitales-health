## New findings

1. **[minor] (introduced by the revision) Section 3.4: the deadline handler and the sweep do not apply the
   "outside the new environment" rule.**
   Line 220 says a channel outside the new environment publishes no freshness transition. But the deadline handler
   "records the transition" whenever it finds a channel stale (line 248), and the sweep "recomputes every channel ...
   and records any transition the deadlines missed" (line 253). A stale deadline scheduled before the switch for
   `ch_co2_cabin_replay` still fires after a switch to `lunar_surface`. Its handler then publishes
   `ChannelFreshnessChanged`, and E24 raises `sensor_quiet` for `co2` in the Moon base environment (E24.md:166): the
   case r3 finding 2 asked to remove. T-E23-079 as written stops the replay but does not leave a pre-switch deadline
   pending.
   **Required change:** in 3.4, make both the deadline handler and the sweep skip channels whose `environment_ids` do
   not include the active environment. Extend T-E23-079 with a deadline scheduled before the switch that falls due
   after it.
   Non-blocking.
