# E23 response to the r3 review (E23.opus-r3.md)

Line numbers are those of `docs/tdd/E23.md` after this revision; "requests" is `docs/requests/E23.md`. All eight
findings were checked against the cited lines and accepted; none is disputed. r1 findings 14 and 19 stay partly
resolved for the contract reasons the review accepted (R-E23-6, R-E23-10).

1. fixed: E23 now ships an interim demo registry, feed file and NASA file copies (with their provenance and a SHA-256
   check against `docs/product/nasa-data/`) under its own path `eval/scenarios/e23/demo/`, loaded only by the demo
   profile in a SIM or REPLAY mission (5.3 "Demo registry and feeds", E23.md:652 to 659; 5.2, E23.md:615 to 616). The
   design names T-E23-029, T-E23-061 and walking skeleton W1.5 as the users of that copy, and says the copy goes when
   E21's fixture carries the same files. The R-E23-15 interim says the same, so no acceptance criterion waits on E21
   (requests R-E23-15).
2. fixed: `on_activated` sets the `activated_at` reference only for channels whose `environment_ids` include the new
   environment (E23.md:215 to 217). A channel outside it keeps its state, appears on no card and publishes no freshness
   transition until an environment listing it is active again (E23.md:220 to 222). T-E23-079 adds a stopped
   station-only replay after a switch to the Moon base, which must raise nothing.
3. fixed: `on_activated` schedules each channel's stale deadline with `uow.schedule` in the activation unit of work
   (E23.md:217). T-E23-079 asserts the deadline, not the sweep, records the transition.
4. fixed: the health poll runs concurrently in its own background task, separate from the sweep, each poll under
   `sensors.adapter_poll_timeout_s` and at most `sensors.adapter_poll_max` adapters (E23.md:630 to 632, 876). A
   non-loopback `health_url` is allowed on a separate integration node over the hub's TLS with the same timeout
   (E23.md:632 to 634). REPLAY's `clock_state` does not use the poll (E23.md:153), and the `unknown` row now applies
   the no-endpoint rule to LIVE and SIM only; R-E23-15's reason says so. T-E23-081 adds a slow adapter that must not
   delay the sweep, and REPLAY with no poll.
5. fixed: the new "Fault notices" paragraph (E23.md:268 to 277) defines health component `sensors` with the four
   codes and `since`, and says what "the health line of R-SYS-9" means everywhere in the design. Request R-E23-16 asks
   SYSTEM.md to add the component and codes to R-SYS-9 and E03 to add their words. The interim is stated: `/v1/health`
   only (the R-SYS-9 interim), no crew sentence, with the crew side kept safe by behavior. The settings row that said
   "card says out of date" now names the code (E23.md:878). Test T-E23-085.
6. fixed: the card's `display_value` and `aggregated_value` under a same-instant conflict come from the variant that
   ranks worse, and the conflict is flagged through health until R-E23-14 (E23.md:319 to 323). `clock_state` is one
   value per input, checked over the longest window among the channel's evaluated limits, or the instant E when all
   are `latest` (E23.md:157 to 159). T-E23-080 and T-E23-081 test both.
7. fixed: the R-E23-3 interim names the registry location of finding 1 (requests line 56).
8. fixed: the demo registry is validated and loaded inside the `mission init` unit of work, and at every later
   start-up once the mode is known, with an audit entry naming its SHA-256; before init no demo channel exists
   (E23.md:183, 653 to 655). T-E23-073 and T-E23-084 cover it.
