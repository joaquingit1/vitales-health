# E23 response to the r2 review (E23.opus-r2.md)

Line numbers are those of `docs/tdd/E23.md` after this revision; "requests" is `docs/requests/E23.md`. Every new
finding was checked against the cited lines and the package before it was accepted; none is disputed. Two owner
rulings arrived between the rounds: decision D8 (replay freshness, already reviewed) and decision D9 (the threshold
edit test is not a safety mapping edit).

## New findings

1. fixed: 3.3 step 4 (E23.md:210 to 214). Activation no longer publishes a `SensorEvaluated` for a channel without an
   evaluable reading in the new environment, which at the activation instant is every channel, so E24 never receives
   an input without a window end, reading or received time. The freshness transition publishes `SensorEvaluated` only
   when the channel has an evaluable reading, and `ChannelFreshnessChanged` with `last_reading_id` `None` otherwise
   (E23.md:254 to 257), which E24's `FreshnessInput` allows. T-E23-049 adds the activation case and a transition with
   no reading.
2. fixed: the cutoff is by observation time for LIVE and SIM and by receive time for REPLAY, stated the same way in
   DE23-13 (E23.md:47), 3.3 (E23.md:215 to 218), 3.4 (E23.md:234 to 235) and 3.7 (E23.md:297 to 299). Readings
   stored before mission init are kept and shown in series but never enter a window, because they precede the
   mission (E23.md:182, 299); T-E23-073 asserts it. T-E23-068 adds a REPLAY case and reruns golden case 5 after
   mission init.
3. fixed: a channel with readings only from before the switch takes `activated_at` as its freshness reference
   (E23.md:228 to 229 for LIVE and SIM, 234 to 235 for REPLAY), so it goes stale at `activated_at +
   stale_after_seconds` and E24 raises `sensor_quiet`; only a channel that never reported is `never_reported`. 3.3
   says the same (E23.md:216 to 218). Test T-E23-079 and a hazard row.
4. fixed: 5.2 (E23.md:609 to 614) defines the path. Each adapter serves a loopback `/health` with an E23 schema
   (`clock` as `synchronized`, `unsynchronized` or `unknown` from its host's time service), and the hub polls every
   registered `health_url` (E23.md:627) every `sensors.adapter_poll_s` with a timeout of
   `sensors.adapter_poll_timeout_s` (E23.md:845 to 846). `clock_state` reads the last poll (E23.md:153 to 155); a SIM
   generator stamps times from its host clock and reports that host's state; an adapter with no endpoint gives
   `unknown`, so its warnings can raise but never clear, which R-E23-15 tells E21 about its interim feeder. Test
   T-E23-081 (healthy SIM `trusted`, lapsed or failed poll `unknown`).
5. fixed by withdrawal: the stuck value check is removed (E23.md:332 to 335); no number is borrowed and
   `insufficient_data` keeps its C13 meaning. R-E23-2 now asks for a stuck criterion (count and span, in observation
   time, with a basis) next to range, saturation and rate (requests R-E23-2). T-E23-025 asserts that the RR-1 golden
   window, with its repeated quantized values, is never flagged.
6. fixed: the sweep runs on its own background timer, not the platform scheduler, and the same tick is a scheduler
   watchdog that marks health `scheduler_lag` when a stored deadline runs late (E23.md:244 to 253). The common mode
   is stated: both run in the hub process; a dead process is covered by the supervisor, the start-up sweep and the
   clients' offline state (E23.md:250 to 253). T-E23-019 adds a stalled scheduler. Setting
   `sensors.scheduler_lag_max_s` (E23.md:847).
7. fixed: where two values disagree at one instant, each limit is evaluated on a low and a high variant of its window;
   every C13 aggregation is monotone in each value, so a crossing on either variant raises and a clear needs both
   (E23.md:300 to 303). Both readings are kept and the adapter is degraded `instant_conflict`, shown through the health
   line of R-SYS-9 (E23.md:366 to 367). Test T-E23-080.
8. fixed: before init every mission command, and `getMission` and `getEnvironment`, answer `INTERNAL_ERROR`
   (retryable) with `current_state.unavailable` naming `mission`, which every operation declares through its
   `default` envelope (E23.md:183 to 184). `INVALID_TRANSITION` is no longer used for it.
9. fixed: `DayLabelPort.inline_label_at(instant)` renders the template of the profile that was active at `instant`,
   through `ProfileDayPort.at(view, instant)` (E23.md:501 to 503). T-E23-010 adds a transit instant after a switch to
   Mars.
10. fixed: the demo gate needs no hub call. Each adapter checks the local deployment profile, and the hub loads the
    demo channels only when its profile is `demo` and the mission mode is SIM or REPLAY (E23.md:603 to 606, 627 to
    630). A failing E29 no longer stops a feed. Test T-E23-084, with E29 absent.
11. fixed: the activation guard uses the fastest of the catalog interval and every registry override, the registry is
    validated against the same bound (E23.md:314 to 315, 627), and the run-time case of a channel faster than
    declared is stated honestly: that limit is not evaluated (neither raises nor clears), the status can be
    `insufficient_data` and health is degraded `window_scan_bound` with the channel named (E23.md:315 to 319). Test
    T-E23-083.
12. fixed: one feed file with one owner. E23 dropped `fixtures/habitat-replay/` from its ownership (section 13,
    E23.md:1006) and consumes E21's `fixtures/demo-mara-fd19/sensors/feeds.json`, whose schema E23 owns (E23.md:596
    to 602). R-E23-15 asks E21 to adopt the schema, hold the NASA file copies with their SHA-256 check and the demo

13. fixed: the R-E23-1 interim now states the decided behavior and cites D8 (requests line 26).
14. fixed: `trace` carries only the channel list and the reading provenance the package defines (E23.md:341 to 343);
    the evaluation-out-of-date state goes through the health line of R-SYS-9 (E23.md:555), not new sentences, and the
