### 3.2 Freshness (`freshness.py`)

`period_seconds(f, calendar)`: `daily` 86 400; `weekly` `calendar.week_days` x 86 400; `monthly` `calendar.month_days`
x 86 400; `every_n_days` `f.n` x 86 400. Any other word is rejected by loader check R-L3.

`skill_record(person, skill, practices, f, calendar, now, label_at) -> SkillRecord`, with `practices` already filtered
to one person, skill, mission and origin mode:

* Cutoff: only practices with `practiced_at <= now` count. A practice dated after `now` (possible only after a backward
  clock correction) is ignored until the clock passes it and is counted in the `practice_after_now` log metric.
* No counted practice: `freshness` `never`, every time field `null`, `source` `null`.
* `last` = the counted practice with the greatest `practiced_at` (ties: `drill`, `event`, `preflight`, then
  `source_ref_id`); `trained_at` = the greatest counted `preflight` time or `null`.
* `due_at` = `last_practiced_at` + `due_after_periods` x period; `stale_at` = `last_practiced_at` +
  `stale_after_periods` x period; `freshness` `fresh` when `now < due_at`, `due` when `due_at <= now < stale_at`,
  `stale` when `now >= stale_at`.
* `last_practiced_label` = `label_at(last_practiced_at)`; `freshness_text` = ui-copy `freshness_words[freshness]`;
  `freshness_basis` and `freshness_to_be_set_by` from the catalog.

Fixture check: practice 2026-11-06T15:20:00Z, `monthly`, `month_days` 30, periods 1 and 2 give `due_at`
2026-12-06T15:20:00Z and `stale_at` 2027-01-05T15:20:00Z (`api/examples/events/page-drill-day12.json`).

A **holder** of a skill (CONTRACTS section 24) is a person whose record is `fresh` or `due` at `now`. Origin rule: role
proposals, holders, readiness and freshness read only practices whose `origin_mode` is the mission's practice mode
(`LIVE` when the mission mode is `LIVE`, otherwise `SIM`). REPLAY never creates a practice; SIM practice never makes a
holder in a LIVE mission (CONTRACTS section 9).

### 3.3 Role engine (`role_engine.py`)

`propose(ctx, rules, candidates, records, kept, now) -> Proposal(assignments, unfilled, officers_passed)`. Deterministic
and independent of input order; IDs are assigned outside the domain. Order of work, CONTRACTS section 24:

1. **Hard exclusions** (`never_subject`, `not_on_eva_for_on_site`): on the roster, not `ctx.subject_person_id`, not
   `on_eva` for an `on_site` role. They apply to every proposal, every kept assignment and every reassign, before
   anything else.
2. **Preference order** among eligible people: awake before asleep (`awake_first`), then, for a skilled need or the
   `freshest_skill_holder` step, the latest `last_practiced_at`, then `roster_index`. No other preference exists,
   except the one tie rule in step 5.
3. **Kept** (section 3.4) assignments are placed first; their people count as holding a role.
4. **`medical_officer`** when the needs list it and no kept assignment fills it: walk `rules.cascade` in order. For
   `officer_rank` r, the roster person with `medical_officer_rank == r`: if ineligible, record `officer_is_subject` or
   `officer_on_eva` and continue; if asleep while a later step yields an awake eligible person, record `officer_asleep`
   and continue; otherwise assign with the step's `reason_code`. For `freshest_skill_holder`, the needed skill is the
   `medical_officer` need's `skill_id`, else the first non-null `skill_id` among the needs in order; assign the eligible
   holder with the greatest `last_practiced_at`; with no needed skill or no eligible holder, the role is `unfilled`. If
   every step passed over only asleep officers, assign the first asleep eligible officer with its step's `reason_code`.
   `officers_passed` lists each officer step passed over and why.
5. **Required needs in flow order, then optional needs** (`one_role_each`: a required need takes people without a role
   first and people with one only when none is free; an optional need takes only people without a role).
   * A need with `skill_id` goes only to an eligible **holder**, the one with the latest `last_practiced_at`
     (`freshest_skill`). With no eligible holder the need is `unfilled`, required or not; a `never`, `stale` or
     (in LIVE) SIM-only person is never proposed for it. Tie between holders with the same `last_practiced_at`: a
     holder who is not `ctx.opener_person_id` comes first, then roster order (request Q2; this tie is the only place
     the opener is read, and it never applies to roles without a skill).
   * A need without `skill_id` goes to the first eligible person in preference order (`free_to_help`).
6. `reason_text` = `reason_phrases[reason_code]` rendered with `{officer_name}`, `{eva_word}`, `{time}`,
   `{skill_short}`, `{day_label}`, `{person_name}`; a value with no source renders ui-copy `designed_states.unknown`.
   `context_reasons[].text` uses the same phrases. `label` from `roles[role_id].label`; `task_text` from the need.

Plan runs (`propose_plan_run`): one need per distinct `skill_id` among the run's skill steps (section 3.4), each
proposed as `rules.plan_step_role` for the freshest eligible holder of that skill, with `context_reasons` for each
medical officer who is unavailable (subject, on a walk, asleep). No holder: `unfilled`, and E28 shows the step
`needs_skill_holder`.

### 3.4 RoleAssignment lifecycle

States (contract): `proposed`, `accepted`, `changed`. A **helper-set** assignment is one with `changed_by` not null.

* `accept`: allowed for the assigned person's own session in `proposed` or `changed`; sets `accepted` and
  `accepted_at`. `changed_by` and `changed_at` stay, so a helper's choice stays marked (CONTRACTS 24: "accept sets
  accepted").
* `reassign` (`person_id`): any visible session (section 10), in any state of a current assignment of an open context;
  in place, as CONTRACTS 24 says: new person, `changed`, `changed_by_helper`, `changed_by`, `changed_at`,
  `accepted_at` null. The target must pass the hard exclusions; skills and `one_role_each` are not checked (any
  eligible person, CONTRACTS 24).
* **Current** assignments of a context: `replaced_by_id` null and `retired_at` null. Only current assignments appear in
  views and broadcasts; replaced and retired rows stay in storage and the audit trail.

On each proposal the engine sorts the context's current assignments into:

| Current assignment | Condition | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `proposed` | anything | replaced when the new proposal differs for its role; else untouched |
| `accepted` or helper-set | person passes the hard exclusions and its `(role_id, skill_id, task_text)` matches an unmatched need of the context | **kept**, never moved (CONTRACTS 24 "never moves a role a helper changed") |
| `accepted` or helper-set | person became the subject or entered `eva` for an `on_site` role | **retired** `ineligible_subject` or `ineligible_eva`; the role is proposed afresh |
| `accepted` or helper-set | no matching need (flow switched, `start_over`, need removed) | **retired** `need_changed`; the new needs are proposed afresh |

Retiring is not moving: EI9 ("never assigns the subject; never assigns someone in an eva overlay to an on-site role")
outranks keeping, and an assignment for a task the flow no longer has must not stay actionable. When the fresh proposal
fills the role, the retired row gets `replaced_by_id`; otherwise the role is listed in `unfilled`. Retirement writes
`role.changed` and an audit entry with the reason; the API representation is request Q13 (interim: the row leaves the
view, which the client refetches on any `role.*` event).

Plan runs: assignments map to steps by skill. A run has at most one current assignment per `skill_id`, and that person
serves every step of the run whose `who` is `skill:<skill_id>`. Marking one step done changes nothing for the others;
the assignment stays until the run closes. A per-step association needs `plan_step_id` on RoleAssignment (Q13).

`allowed_actions` is computed per session: `accept` for the assigned person in `proposed` or `changed`; `reassign` for
any visible session; empty when the context is closed, the assignment is not current or the session cannot see it.

### 3.5 Proposal inputs and invalidation

A proposal is a pure function of its **input snapshot**: context snapshot (subject, opener, open, flow or skill steps),
roster, active overlays, holder sets of the needed skills (with each holder's `last_practiced_at`), mission practice
mode, release selection and the current assignments. The application recomputes and writes only the difference from
the current assignments; the same snapshot writes nothing, so duplicate event delivery is harmless and no
deduplication by session revision exists.

| Input changes | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Context reaches a flow, flow switch, `start_over`, subject, helpers, close | `help.session_changed` |
| Plan run opened, skill step done, closed | `plan_run.changed`, `plan_run.step_done` |
| Overlay starts or ends | `overlay.changed` (hard exclusion applied in the handler, section 4.1) |
| A practice changes holders | E31's own `skill.practiced` |
| A holder becomes `stale` with no event | freshness timer at the earliest `stale_at` among holders of needed skills in open contexts |
| Active `role-rules`, `skills-catalog`, `ui-copy` (help sessions only) | `config.release_activated` |
| Mission mode or environment | `environment.activated` |
| Roster by mission day | mission day boundary tick |
| Clock correction | `MissionClockCorrected`: recompute every open context, re-arm timers |
| Restart | startup sweep of every open context |

### 3.6 Drill state

`running → completed` (`POST /v1/drills/{id}/complete`), `running → abandoned` (automatic at the end of the mission
day it started on, Q4). Final states are final. `stores_measurements` is the constant `false`.

Scenario and skills (CONTRACTS 24 "a scenario of skills-catalog.json, its skills"; the catalog nests scenarios under
each skill): every requested skill must list `scenario_id` in its own `drill_scenarios`; a skill that does not is
`INVALID_VALUE` at `/skill_ids/<i>`. Credited skills are exactly the requested skills. Needed supplies are the union of
`needs_supplies` of that scenario entry under every credited skill.

Completion writes, in one transaction: the drill row; one `drill_participant` row per participant with `completed`
and `core_fresh_at_completion` (section 3.7); one `skill_practice` row per completing participant and credited skill
(`source` `drill`, `source_ref_id` the drill, `practiced_at` = `completed_at` = hub time of the commit); a
`routine_completion_due` row when an occurrence is linked and its person completed (section 5.3); `drill.changed` and
one `skill.practiced` per practice row.

### 3.7 Practice patches (`practice.py`)

Computed on read from `drill_participant` rows of one person, one mission and one origin mode, with `completed`
true, ordered by `(completed_at, drill_id)`. Event and preflight practices never count (CONTRACTS 24 "counts toward the
practice patches"; I-D3).

* `first_drill`: the first such row. `drill_count` threshold n: the nth row.
* `skill_fresh`: the first row whose `core_fresh_at_completion` is true. That flag is computed once, inside the
  completion transaction, as: every `core_skill_ids` skill has a `drill` practice of this person, mission and origin,
  dated at or before `completed_at`, whose record is `fresh` at `completed_at`, under the release active at that
  completion. Later practice and later configuration edits therefore never backdate an award; an edit to
  `core_skill_ids` or a cadence applies to completions after it.
* Thresholds and designs come from the active `participation-rules.practice`. Award ID = `ppa_` + first 16 hex of
  SHA-256 of `person|mission|origin|kind|threshold`. No duration, camera value or guided check result is an input.

### 3.8 Task, competence and authority

Three separate concepts (owner decision, section 14 Q16):

| Concept | What makes it | What it never does |
|---|---|---|
| Task assignment | a current RoleAssignment | grant clinical authority by itself |
| Demonstrated competence | practices: preflight, drills, attributable guided checks | grant a role or authority by itself; a drill alone never makes a medical officer |
| Clinical authorization | rank 1 or 2 officer on the roster; or **acting medical officer** | follow from skills or patches |

Acting medical officer: in a help session, a person is acting while all hold: (a) a current `medical_officer`
assignment names them; (b) they accepted it from their own session; (c) at that acceptance every `officer_rank`
cascade step was unavailable (absent, the subject, on a walk or asleep), recorded as `acting_since`; (d) since then no
officer has become eligible and awake, the assignment was not replaced or retired, and the session is open. When (d)
fails, `acting_ended_at` is set. Start and end each write an audit entry with the assignment, person, session and the
officers' reasons. E31 serves the status through `ActingMedicalOfficerPort`; E26 (and E15) decide what an acting officer
may disposition (Q16). Plan runs have no acting officer (`plan_step_role` is not `medical_officer`).

**Exclusions in the handler.** `OnContextEvent` and `OnOverlayChanged` read the subject from the HelpSession payload
and the person, overlay kind and state from the ActivityOverlay payload, and retire every current assignment that now
breaks a hard exclusion in the same transaction that marks the context for recompute. No port call is needed, so a
timeout can never leave the subject or a walker in an on-site role (I-R1, I-R2).

**Event practice** (`RecordGuidedCheckPractice`, E30 X12): a GuidedCheck in `checked` with `result` `agree` or
`no_reference`, `drill_id` null and `origin_mode` equal to the mission practice mode gives its `helper` one practice
of its `skill_id`: `source` `event`, `source_ref_id` the guided check, `practiced_at` the event's `occurred_at`.
`disagree`, `retry`, `cancelled` and drill checks give none; a retried check yields one practice at most (unique key).
Holding, accepting or being reassigned a role gives no practice, and closing a help session gives none: the contract's
"the person did the skill in a help session" needs an attestation the API lacks (Q5).

**Drill validation** (`StartDrill`), in order: the session may start it (crew sessions only as a participant;
`crew_medical_officer` and station any); each skill exists and lists the scenario (3.6); each participant is on the
roster; `origin_mode` is the mission practice mode (`OriginMismatch`); open drills fewer than `roles.max_open_drills`;
every needed supply aboard in the active environment (`SuppliesNotAboard`); `routine_occurrence_id`, when set, is
`due` or `later`, its person is a participant, and its item `skill_ids` include every requested skill
(`InvalidDrill` at `/routine_occurrence_id`).

**Completion** (`CompleteDrill`): omitted `participant_ids` means every participant (`DrillComplete` description); when
present it must be a non-empty subset of the participants (`BadParticipants`). The actor must be a participant, the
starter, `crew_medical_officer` or station. The completion is the actor's attestation, recorded in the audit entry with
the completing participants. A drill whose mission day has ended is abandoned first and the command returns
`NotRunning` with the abandoned drill.

**Role actions** (`ActOnRole`), inside one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`: idempotency lookup; load the assignment; it must belong
to the path's context (`RoleNotFound` otherwise, so an ID from another context reveals nothing); the session must see
the context (section 10); the context must be open (`ContextClosed`); the assignment must be current (`NotCurrent`
with the current assignment of that role); `If-Match` against its revision; for `accept`, the session person must be
the assigned person (`NotAssignee`); for `reassign`, the target must pass the hard exclusions under the roster and
overlays read in this transaction (`NotEligible`); write, audit, outbox; a pending recompute of the context sees the
new revision. Proposals use the same lock and compare revisions before writing, so a racing proposal never overwrites a
role action and two clients racing on one assignment get one success and one `StaleRevision`.

Accepting a `medical_officer` assignment evaluates 3.8 (c) in the same transaction and writes `acting_since` and the
audit entry when it holds.

### 5.2 Recompute worker

`role_recompute` is the durable work list: one row per context needing a proposal (PK, so its size is bounded by the
open contexts). Handlers upsert it in their own transaction; an in-memory signal (bounded, coalescing) only wakes the
worker, so a full signal queue loses nothing. The worker, an asyncio task holding no transaction while it calls ports,
takes up to `roles.recompute_batch` rows whose `next_attempt_at <= now`, reads the snapshot through the ports, runs
`propose`, writes the diff and deletes the row in one transaction.

* Failure (`PortUnavailable`, invalid snapshot): `attempts` + 1, `next_attempt_at` = now + `roles.retry_base_seconds`
  x 2^min(attempts, 6), capped at `roles.retry_max_seconds`. Retries need no new event and continue while the context
  is open.
* Overdue: when `requested_at` is more than `roles.proposal_overdue_seconds` in the past, the context's `proposed` assignments are
  retired `degraded` (roles listed `unfilled`), `degraded_at` set, audit `roles.proposal_degraded`, health check
  degraded. Accepted and helper-set assignments stay: they are human decisions already checked against the exclusions
  by the handlers. The degraded marker clears on the next success. Q13 asks for a view field; interim the roles show
  unfilled.
* Capacity: when `proposal_count` reaches `roles.max_proposals_per_context`, the context is degraded the same way and
  no further engine proposal is written; reassign still works.
* Startup: abandon sweep (5.4), reconcile `routine_completion_due`, upsert a row for every open context (E26 and E28
  open IDs), arm the timers.

### 5.3 Routine completion obligation

A completion with a linked occurrence whose person completed writes `routine_completion_due` (`pending`) in the drill's
transaction. `CompleteLinkedOccurrences` calls `RoutineCompletionPort.complete(occurrence_id, completed_at, actor)`
with the drill's own `completed_at`; on `False` it reads `drill_occurrence`: `done` settles `done` (an earlier attempt
landed but its response was lost), `missed` or `skipped` settles `refused` (logged, never forced: misses are never
filled in); a timeout backs off as in 5.2. Startup reconciles every `pending` row. A crash before the drill commit
leaves nothing (the client retries with the same key); a crash after it leaves a `pending` row. A full disk fails the
drill transaction as a whole (`INTERNAL_ERROR`, retryable) and nothing is half written.

### 5.4 Time

The `day_end` timer holds the earliest `MissionPort.day_end(started_at)` of running drills; `AbandonEndedDrills` sets
`abandoned_at` = that day end (not the late processing time). It runs at the timer, at startup (restart across
midnight), after a `MissionClockCorrected` (forward jump) and inline before any drill command. A backward correction
never reopens a drill. The `freshness` timer holds the earliest future `stale_at` among holders of needed skills of open
contexts; at it, those contexts are marked for recompute. Both timers are re-armed after every write that changes them,
at startup and after a clock correction. Preflight rows with `trained_at` after the import time are rejected; event and
drill practice times are hub times, never client times.

### 5.5 Preflight import

`uv run crew-health skills import-preflight --file <path>` (CLI on E20's entry point). JSON validated by
`roles/adapters/preflight.schema.json`; at most `roles.preflight_max_rows` rows and `roles.preflight_max_bytes`; every
person on the roster, every skill in the catalog, `trained_at` not in the future; `origin_mode` `LIVE` requires mission
mode `LIVE` and `--attest <operator>`, otherwise `SIM`. All or nothing. Demo preflight data lives only in
`fixtures/demo-mara-fd19/` and loads through the fixture profile.

## 11. Performance and resources

| Budget | Target |
|---|---|
| Proposal after `help.session_changed` reaches a flow | p95 under 200 ms (D41 role rows under 500 ms end to end) |
| `GET .../roles`, `GET /v1/me/skills`, `GET /v1/crew/readiness` | p95 under 100 ms with 8 crew, 20 skills and 100 000 practices |
| `POST /v1/drills`, `.../complete` | p95 under 150 ms |
| Help under load | E26 open, answer and tick keep their budgets while 50 contexts recompute continuously (T-E31-036) |

Work per request: a SkillRecord is two indexed single-row lookups (latest counted practice, latest preflight); my
skills is catalog size times that; readiness is roster size times catalog size; patches are at most three indexed
single-row lookups per patch design. No read replays history, so practice volume does not change latency. Memory: one
proposal holds at most roster x roles candidates; the recompute worker holds one batch. Background work (recompute,
routine completion, timers) runs as asyncio tasks that never hold a transaction across a port call and yield after each
context.
