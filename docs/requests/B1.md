# Contract change requests from B1 (numerical core)

Each entry gives the proposed change, the reason and the interim behavior that the code implements now. Code that
depends on an entry carries a comment naming it (`docs/requests/B1.md R-nn`). Modules: exposure, microclimate,
kinetics, uncertainty, update, decision, pipeline, store, fit.

---

## R-01. Spec 5.3: D2 and D4 inverses without numerics settings

**Proposed change (spec 5.3, `conversion_from_theta`):** state that `numerics` is required for D2 and D4 and that
calling either without it raises `ModelDomainError`.

**Reason:** the signature makes `numerics` optional, but the D2 and D4 inverses need `inverse_absolute_tolerance`,
`small_alpha_series_threshold` and `series_max_terms` from engine-settings. A built-in default would be a tunable number
in code.

**Interim:** as proposed (`kinetics._numerics_values`).

## R-02. Spec 5.9: where the ODE reference starts for Avrami and diffusion kinds

**Proposed change (spec 5.9):** "Kinds whose f is zero or singular at alpha = 0 (D1 to D4, A2, A3, A4, An) start the
ODE at alpha_s = 1e-9 and consume g(alpha_s) of Theta from the start of the history, so the end point corresponds to the
full Theta."

**Reason:** an ODE started exactly at alpha = 0 never leaves it for the Avrami kinds (f(0) = 0), and the diffusion
kinds are singular there. The spec names the offset only for the diffusion kinds.

**Interim:** as proposed (`kinetics.reference_alpha_ode`).

## R-03. Spec 5.4: J quadrature branch, Ei underflow and the Gauss-Legendre ladder

**Proposed change (spec 5.4):** add a third branch: "when |x| (1 - e^-delta) <= 1 and delta <= 0.1, J is computed by
Gauss-Legendre quadrature of the integrand (16 nodes, fewer for small max(span, delta) per the ladder
(1e-5, 2), (1e-3, 3), (5e-3, 4), (0.03, 6), (0.1, 8))". State also that when x e^-delta is below the smallest normal
float, Ei(x e^-delta) is taken from its leading terms gamma + ln|x| - delta (exact in float64 there), so a long segment
never underflows to Ei(0) = -inf.

**Reason:** for x < 0 the alternating series loses up to e^|x| and the Ei difference up to 1/(|x| delta); both miss the
1e-12 accuracy of 5.4 on short segments of slow packages. The quadrature has no cancellation for either sign of x. The
ladder rungs were measured against mpmath at 40 digits over both signs of x and delta from 1e-6 m to m (worst 9.4e-16
relative). It is a speed choice only (about 3 times faster for sealed-like packages, tau_m of years).

**Interim:** as proposed (`kinetics._humidity_integral_array`).

## R-04. Spec 5.3: `theta_from_conversion` uses the closed forms

**Proposed change (spec 5.3):** state that `theta_from_conversion` (g) uses the closed forms of the table for every
alpha, with `expm1`/`log1p` where they apply, and that the small-alpha series is only part of the D2 and D4 inverses.

**Reason:** the series threshold lives in `numerics`, which `theta_from_conversion` does not receive. The closed forms
written with `log1p` keep the round trip conversion_from_theta(theta_from_conversion(alpha)) within 1e-12 relative from
alpha = 1e-12 (tests/property/test_kinetics_properties.py), and the B3 oracle compares them with mpmath.

**Interim:** as proposed.

## R-05. Spec 8.2: extrapolation widening applies to thermal pathways only

**Proposed change (spec 8.2):** "The widening terms z_T s_T d_T and z_RH s_RH d_RH apply to thermal pathways; light and
radiation pathways have no temperature or humidity extrapolation distance."

**Reason:** d_T and d_RH are distances from a pathway's validated temperature and RH range. Light and radiation
pathways have no Arrhenius or humidity term, so the distance has no meaning for them.

**Interim:** as proposed: the widening enters only the thermal rate exponent (`kinetics._thermal_parts`); d_T and
d_RH of light and radiation pathways are still computed for the range flags and placards.

## R-06. `NominalPathwayTheta` gains per-segment members

**Proposed change (types.NominalPathwayTheta):** add `segment_theta`, `segment_d_rh_percent` and
`segment_rh_in_max_percent` (tuples, one value per lot segment, default empty).

**Reason:** `placards_for` receives no microclimate trace. Without per-segment values it cannot state the humidity
extent, the time spent and the share of a placard exactly (8.4).

**Interim:** added with empty defaults, so existing constructors keep working (`kinetics.NominalPathwayTheta`).

## R-07. Spec 9.7: a fixed zone rate multiplier enters as ln(mu)

**Proposed change (spec 9.7, zone variables):** replace "ln m = mu + sigma z (fixed: mu exactly)" with "lognormal:
ln m = mu + sigma z (mu, sigma in ln units); fixed: ln m = ln(mu), since a fixed prior's mu is the value itself".

**Reason:** `ground_storage` declares a fixed multiplier with mu = 1. Read literally, "mu exactly" puts ln m = 1, a rate
multiplied by e.

**Interim:** as proposed (`kinetics.ln_rate_multiplier`).

## R-08. Degradant percent needs the API molar mass

**Proposed change (types.StabilityModel or spec 6):** either add `api_molar_mass_g_per_mol` to `StabilityModel`, or
state in spec 6 that `evaluate_draws` reports degradant on a molar basis (percent of label in moles) and the pipeline
converts with M_degradant / M_API from the formulary.

**Reason:** spec 6 reports degradant as a percent of the label API mass, which needs both molar masses. The model
carries only the degradant's.

**Interim:** the second option. `evaluate_draws` reports the molar basis; the pipeline multiplies by
M_degradant / M_API (`ingredient.molar_mass_g_per_mol`) and traces `degradant_mass_ratio:<id>`. When the degradant's
molar mass is unknown the molar basis is kept and the trace says so.

## R-09. Spec 8.1: share outside the validated range is share weighted

**Proposed change (spec 8.1):** "The outside share is sum over pathways of (pathway share of nominal Theta) x (fraction
of that pathway's nominal Theta accrued in segments outside its validated range)."

**Reason:** the spec gives the flag but not how several pathways combine. Weighting by share keeps a minor pathway
outside its range from dominating.

**Interim:** as proposed (`kinetics.RangeAssessment.share_outside`).

## R-10. Spec 3.4: the declared value of a lognormal prior is its median

**Proposed change (spec 3.4):** "A declared quantity takes the prior's mu for a normal or fixed prior and exp(mu), the
median, for a lognormal prior."

**Reason:** a lognormal mu is a logarithm; using it as the value would put ln(dose) where a dose is expected.

**Interim:** as proposed (`exposure._declared_value`).

## R-11. `AdaptiveResult.batch_statistics`

**Reason:** the `monte_carlo` placard (8.4) reports 2 s / sqrt(h) of u(y), which needs the per-batch statistics.

**Interim:** added with an empty default.

## R-13. Spec 12.1: observations after as_of are dropped

**Proposed change (spec 12.1):** "Assays and inspections dated after as_of are dropped like readings (never used, never

**Reason:** 1.2 says later data never change a past result. Rejecting them would make one inputs file unusable for its

**Interim:** as proposed (`pipeline.validate_inputs`).

## R-14. Spec 11.4: forecast projection details

**Proposed change (spec 11.4):**

* A `repeat_last_window` zone with no recorded segment (as_of at arrival) falls back to `declared_constant`, and raises
  `ConfigReferenceError` when the zone declares nothing.
* The march detects a periodic window when the microclimate state at a window start equals the state at its end
  within one float64 epsilon relative (high and low RH_in and every unit-step response). From then on every whole
  window repeats the stored one: Theta grows by the window's Theta per repetition, the first window whose end potency
  is below the limit is found per draw by bisection on the window count, and the crossing is located inside it.
* A last window cut by the horizon is evaluated exactly over its remaining length (no rounding to whole windows).

**Reason:** the spec describes the projection but not these edge cases, and each changes forecast times.

**Interim:** as proposed (`pipeline._projection`, `_periodic`, `_march`).

## R-17. Spec 9.5: free-group trace entries and measured blocks

**Proposed change (spec 9.5):** name the trace entries `free_share_delta_ln:<pathway_id>`,
`free_share_method_sd_ln:<pathway_id>` and `free_share_pi:<pathway_id>` (unit "1"), and state that a pathway with a
measured ln k* block is never a member of F even if it carries `free_share`, and that a free member whose block has no
ln k* or a `shared_weight` other than 1, or a non-finite or negative `method_sd_ln`, raises `ModelDomainError`.

**Reason:** 9.5 says "the trace reports delta_i, s_m,i and pi_i" without names, and says the term never applies to
measured models without saying what a measured block that carries `free_share` means. With w other than 1 the member's
own ln k column is already used by its draw, so reusing it as z_F would correlate the two.

**Interim:** as proposed (`uncertainty.free_group`, `uncertainty._add_free_share_term`, `pipeline._free_share_trace`).

## R-18. Spec 10.4: check conflicts before running the first pass to `max_batches`

**Proposed change (spec 10.4):** "Conflicts are checked on the draws of the first `min_batches` batches; a conflict
switches to the re-anchor pass at once."

**Reason:** with a conflicting assay the weights concentrate on few draws, so the weighted first pass seldom reaches
the ESS floor and can run to `max_batches` (100 batches of 10^4 draws) before the conflict check of 10.3 runs on all its
draws. A conflicting assay can then cost up to about 100 times a normal estimate, and that first pass is discarded by
the re-anchor.

**Interim:** the spec as written (conflict checked on all first-pass draws).

## R-19. Smaller unstated choices (no code comment; listed so the spec can adopt them)

* **B < 0 in every evaluation.** The low RH variant (outside and internal) is used for a pathway with B < 0 in
  `evaluate_draws`, in nominal Thetas and in the forecast march alike (3.5 names it only for worst-case fills).
* **Possibly frozen.** For liquid dose forms (solution, suspension) a temperature gap-filled segment whose
  `GapRecord.fill_value_low` is below the freezing point is "possibly frozen" (8.3); solids never are.
* **Radiation method.** The radiation report's `method` comes from the radiolysis pathway's `ComputedDetail.method`
  (g_value_direct or g_value_indirect), else "none".
* **Placard units and extents.** temperature K (degrees outside the range), humidity %RH, time extrapolation the ratio
  age / max_study_duration (unit "1"), frozen and possibly frozen K below the freezing point, phase change K above the
  threshold, deliquescence %RH above the threshold (largest high-variant RH_in of a segment's start and end), Monte
  Carlo percent (2 s / sqrt(h) of u(y)), coverage the longest gap in s.
* **Time-extrapolation share.** Theta inside a segment that straddles the cutoff is taken as uniform in time when the
  placard's share is computed (display only).
* **`within_specification` reason.** It has severity `info`; `degradant_profile_not_reviewed` is an info reason that
  the decision takes from the estimate's flags (the pipeline sets it from the formulary).
* **Series pins.** An estimate pins only the series of the zones in the lot's custody up to as_of (sorted by
  series_id), so a reading in an unrelated zone never changes its seed.
* **`declared_segment`.** Dose rate, illuminance and UV that a zone does not declare are 0 (`not_applicable`, 3.4);
  temperature and RH that are not declared raise `ConfigReferenceError`.
* **Segment chunking.** `evaluate_draws` evaluates 256 segments per pass (memory bound, 12.5). It changes no result
  beyond summation order and is fixed so runs are bit identical.
* **D2 and D4 inverse.** The safeguarded Newton step bisects when g' is infinite (alpha = 1), where a Newton step never
  moves and would pass the step test without converging.

## R-20. Spec 14.3: AICc when n <= p + 1 (TV-H-5)

**Proposed change (spec 14.3 and `fit.StudyFit`):** make `aic`, `aicc` and `bic` of `StudyFit` `float | None`: AICc is
None when n <= p + 1 (and AIC and BIC None when RSS_w = 0); `fit_stability_study` then returns the fit, and
`select_model` raises `InputError` when a candidate has no AICc. Alternatively keep `InputError` and drop TV-H-5 from
the 14.3 vector list (it is plausibility only).

**Reason:** TV-H-5 lots have four points (t0 and three 6-month conditions) for (ln k*, Ea, B), so n = p + 1 and the
AICc correction 2p(p+1)/(n - p - 1) divides by zero. +inf would rank the fit last, but spec 1.6 forbids infinity in an
output. The spec lists TV-H-5 under 14.3 and also defines AICc for every fit, so the two cannot both hold. Same issue
as B3-R20.

**Interim:** `InputError` (`fit.fit_stability_study`); B3's `tests/vectors/test_vectors.py::test_tv_h_5_plausibility`
fails with a message naming B3-R20 until this is decided.

## R-21. Spec 12.5: the 60 s demo budget is out of reach by about two orders of magnitude

**Proposed change (spec 12.5 or 9.9):** decide one of: (a) restate the budget per lot and assessment time; (b) allow
a smaller `n_dig` (1) for operational assessments, which cuts the batch count of wide posteriors; (c) allow the
segment chunks of `evaluate_draws` to run on worker threads (each chunk's partial sums added in chunk order, so results
stay bit identical); (d) sanction a coarser history grid for old segments (an approximation, so it would need its own
accuracy rule).

**Reason (measured, demo fixture, real B2 models, one process, machine shared with two other builders):** at the
first assessment time (day 100, about 4,800 segments per lot) `assess_lot` takes 38 s for lot-apap-001 (2 batches of
9.2 s each, the repackaged bag's J quadrature dominating, plus a 19 s forecast) and 288 s for lot-pmz-inj-001 (51
batches of 4.1 s, probably because the promethazine free group with s_m = 2.3 ln units makes the posterior wide, so the
n_dig = 2 tolerance on the interval ends needs many batches; plus a 74 s forecast). Cost per batch is segments x draws x pathways and grows linearly
with mission age (day 400 has about 19,200 segments), and the batch count is set by JCGM 101 7.9 at n_dig = 2. The
full demo (13 lots, 3 times) through `assess_inventory` therefore takes hours, so B3's end-to-end and scenario tests
(15.4, 15.6) cannot run in a normal test session.

**Interim:** nothing changed; the cost is reported. The engine is correct but slow.

## Fit and store (spec 14, 12.3)

- **R-FIT-1.** Change: add to `fit.StudyFit`, after `converged`, keyword-defaulted members `n_observations: int | None`,
  `temperature_min_k`, `temperature_max_k`, `rh_min_percent`, `rh_max_percent`, `max_time_s`, `order_n`, `alpha0`,
  `avrami_n`, `initial_percent` (all `float | None = None`), filled by `fit_stability_study`. Reason: spec 14.5 has
  `model_document` write the study range, `max_study_duration_s`, `fit.n` and the kind parameters, and the StudyFit of
  the contract carries none of them. Interim: added exactly these, existing fields unchanged.
- **R-FIT-2.** Change: add keyword-only `alpha0: float | None = None`, `avrami_n: float | None = None` and
  `numerics: NumericsSettings | None = None` to `fit_stability_study`. Reason: B1 and An cannot be fitted without their
  kind parameter (VS-2 runs `select_model` over every kind), and the D2 and D4 inverses need the engine-settings
  numerics (R-01).
- **R-FIT-3.** Change: add keyword-only `initial_percent: float | None = None` to `fit_stability_study`. When
  `fit_initial` is false, C0 is that value, else the mean of the time-zero observations, else 100 % of label claim.
  Reason: spec 14.3 does not say what C0 is when it is not fitted.
- **R-FIT-4.** Change: write into spec 14.3 the start values and identifiability checks as implemented.
  - Per group (sorted), Theta of each pull = g(1 - C/C0) for the fitted kind, using pulls with conversion in (0, 1);
    then k_g = sum(t Theta) / sum(t^2); then OLS of ln k_g on (1, x, RH - RH*) on unit-norm columns, minimum norm when
    under-determined. With no usable group the start is ln k* = -ln(t_max), Ea = B = 0.
  - Checks: at least 2 temperatures; at least 2 RH values when fitting B; at least one pull after time zero;
    sd_percent on all observations or none; absolute weights require sd. All raise `InputError`.
  - Reason: the spec only says "from two-stage starts".
- **R-FIT-5.** Change: spec 14.5 says "review_state draft", but `StabilityModelDoc` has no `review_state` (it is a
  document-header member). `model_document` sets `status` "reproduces", `basis` "derived", `study` null and `covers` [],
  and says review_state draft in `note`. It requires a non-empty `source_ids` (`InputError` otherwise), because the
  schema requires sources for derived values. It drops `c0_percent`, which is not a ParameterName, from the parameter
  vector. It writes `reference_rh_percent` null and an RH range of 0 to 100 when there is no humidity term. It validates
  through a stability-models wrapper document. Reason: the spec and the schema disagree on where review_state lives,
  and the remaining choices are unstated.
- **R-FIT-6.** Change: when all ln k are equal (TSS = 0, so RSS = 0), `fit_arrhenius_two_stage` reports r2 = 1.0 instead
  of 0/0. Reason: `ArrheniusFit.r2` is a non-null float, and NaN is forbidden by spec 1.6.
- **R-FIT-7.** Change: in the free-intercept log-linear fit, `c0_se_percent` is computed by the delta method,
  se(C0) = C0 x se(intercept). Reason: spec 14.2 gives no formula.
- **R-FIT-8.** Change: state in spec 14.3 how the fit ends.
  - least_squares status 0 (evaluation limit) is returned with `converged` false.
  - A status below 0, or a non-finite estimate, raises `ConvergenceError`.
  - RSS_w = 0 raises `ComputationError`, because AIC would be -inf.
  - A singular J^T W J (rank checked on unit-norm columns) raises `ComputationError`; no pseudo-inverse is used.
  - Reason: the spec defines the covariance and information criteria but not these failure cases.
- **R-STORE-1.** Change: spec 12.3 should state:
  - Sequence numbers start at 0, exposed as `store.FIRST_SEQUENCE`.
  - `append` raises `InputError` for bad metadata: empty kind, actor or reason; a recorded_at_utc that is not ISO with
    Z; a payload that is not a JSON object or not canonical-serializable.
  - `records()` requires canonical lines, the exact eight keys, 64 lower-case hex hashes and a trailing newline, and
    raises `StoreError` otherwise.
  - A missing or empty file is an empty store.
  - Reason: the spec names `StoreError` only for a broken chain and leaves these points open.

## Spec findings (no code depends on them)

* **11.7 monotonicity.** "Lowering every draw never improves a state" is false when the estimate starts above the upper
  limit: lowering it can bring it back inside. Proposed wording: "on estimates whose upper quantile is at or below the
  upper limit". The property test (tests/property/test_statistics_properties.py) uses that restriction.
* **TV-Q1E-5** targets "decision documentation" without naming where (B3-R28). The `pharmacy.decision` module
  docstring now states the order (slopes, then intercepts) and the level 0.25; tests/unit/core/test_decision.py and
  B3's vector test check that docstring.
* **TV-KIN-4 and TV-H-5** are degradant tables, while `fit_stability_study` models remaining drug, so the tests convert
  to 100 - degradant with C0 fixed at 100. The spec should say this is how degradant data enter.
* **TV-RAD-1** is in ug/mL and is passed as `percent`; this works because the log-linear fit does not depend on scale.
* **TV-H-2** publishes 1.2 for a value that evaluates to 1.2962013032294577 from its own inputs; the test asserts the
  computed value (known mismatch).
* **15.7 one batch per replicate.** `run_adaptive` requires `min_batches >= 2`, so "one batch of M draws (adaptive
  stopping off)" cannot be set through engine-settings. The coverage study swaps `uncertainty.run_adaptive` for a
  one-batch run in the test. Proposed: say so in 15.7, or allow `min_batches = max_batches = 1` as "stopping off".
* **15.7 truth.** "Synthetic truth drawn from the engine's own priors ... history from the simulator" does not say
  whether the truth is evaluated on the simulator's truth series or on the history the engine builds from the readings.
  The study evaluates the truth draw (its zone offsets included) on the engine's history, which makes coverage a check of
  the posterior itself; the truth-series version is VS-3 (15.8).

## Finding for B2, B3 and the coordinator (demo, day 100)

* With B2's models the demo expectation `x-apap-002-in-spec` (state in_specification at 2028-04-19) fails: the engine
  gives do_not_use. Potency is fine (q05 = 91.3), but the product-specific toxic degradant 4-aminophenol (limit
  0.15 % of label) is predicted at a mean of 1.8 % (q05 0.70, p_above_limit 1.0): the hydrolysis pathway carries 62 %
  of a median loss of about 3.8 %, and 109.13 / 151.16 = 0.722 converts it to mass. The arithmetic is consistent, so the
  conflict is between the class-prior hydrolysis rate, the 0.15 % limit and the expectation. The other two day-100
  expectations (`x-asa-before-excursion`, `x-epi-order`) pass. Days 250 and 400 were not run (R-21).

## Finding for B3 (oracle)

* `tests/oracle/kinetics.py` `rate_function` for D2 at alpha near 1e-43 cancels: it evaluates ln(1 - alpha) at 50 digits,
  which leaves about 6 significant digits, and disagrees with the engine by 8.2e-9 relative (alpha = 5.833587797210152e-44:
  engine 1.714210936326764e+43, oracle 1.7142109504535987e+43). Using `mpmath.log1p(-alpha)` removes it; the engine
  value is the accurate one.
