# Requests from E24: Warnings engine

R-E24-1 to R-E24-23 are section 14 of the frozen design (`docs/tdd/E24.md`), brought in line with decisions D1, D2
and Q9 in the same way as the binding corrections of `docs/build/E24/PLAN.md`. R-E24-24 to R-E24-33 come from a later
design revision (branch `e24-revision`), which stayed off main because the design is frozen (AGENTS.md 5). Section
numbers such as "3.12" refer to the frozen design in entries 1 to 23 and to the revision in entries 24 to 33; test IDs
above T-E24-138 exist only in the revision. Where an interim here differs from a binding correction of the build plan,
the build plan wins. Each entry states its target, the proposal, the reason and the interim. No interim switches off a
safety check, a validation or an acceptance criterion. Where no safe interim exists, the feature stays off and the
acceptance row reports failing (design 12.1).
